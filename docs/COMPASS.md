# DevOps Asiana Compass

This is the durable orientation document for DevOps Asiana. It prevents the active frontier from being chosen merely because the latest compiler is the most familiar abstraction.

## What the Firm is

DevOps Asiana is a documentation-first institutional operating system for a Philippine General Partnership accepting professional responsibility for critical financial infrastructure. It combines law-firm-style partnership governance, professional-services accountability, SRE operational discipline, deterministic institutional compilation, repository-backed truth, and evidence-backed operations.

The Firm is distinct from the surrounding ecosystem:

```text
3neti → technology / intellectual property
ODTI → platform / scheme / commercialization
Client → infrastructure and data ownership
DevOps Asiana → delegated professional technology operations
```

The governing chain is:

```text
Partnership Agreement
        ↓
Policies
        ↓
Procedures / Runbooks
        ↓
Evidence / Audit Trails
```

## Constitutional projection

The active vertical slice is:

```text
Canonical Institutional Sources
             ↓
    Partnership Definition
             ↓
      ResolvePartnership
             ↓
      ResolvedPartnership
             ↓
  Validate · expose gaps · counsel review
             ↓
  CompilePartnershipAgreement
             ↓
    Working Partnership Agreement
             ↓
          Firm Map
```

`ResolvedPartnership` is the intermediate representation. The Partnership Agreement is a deterministic projection, not canonical truth. The compiler does not call an LLM, approve a source, execute an Agreement, or assert legal validity.

AI may draft changes to canonical repository artifacts. Humans constitute the Partnership. Meaningful lifecycle states remain distinct: Proposed, Reviewed, Accepted, Compiled, and later—only when supported—Approved for Execution, Executed, and Effective.

## What future agents should build next

1. Keep the Partnership Agreement projection deterministic and readable.
2. Make unresolved formation decisions and counsel-review items more useful to human decision-makers.
3. Keep the Firm Map and Agreement projection derived from the same `ResolvedPartnership`.
4. Add compilation comparison only when an actual institutional change requires it.
5. Advance toward commencement only through explicit, counsel-aware, evidenced sources.

## What future agents should avoid next

Do not build a generic legal editor, CRM, ticketing system, workflow engine, audit platform, billing system, compensation engine, customer portal, infrastructure orchestrator, or additional Control / Evidence compiler without a real institutional use case. Do not infer legal execution, commencement, authority, capital, Client acceptance, or operational responsibility from compilation, Git history, login, title, or technical activity.
