# Policy Exceptions

> **Exceptions are explicit, temporary, approved, and reviewable.**

A Policy Exception shall identify the exact policy version and requirement, affected subject and scope, business reason, assessed risk, compensating controls, accountable owner, approver and authority, effective date, expiry, review date, status, and evidence.

An exception cannot authorize conduct prohibited by law or exceed the approver's authority. It should use the narrowest scope and shortest period that addresses the need. Renewal is a new affirmative decision, not an automatic extension.

Expired exceptions cease to authorize deviation. Closure must record whether the condition was remediated, policy changed, activity ceased, or a new exception approved. Repeated exceptions should trigger review of the underlying control or operating design.
