# Cultural Constitution

These rules are operating constraints, not marketing values.

> **No Client Without Acceptance.**
>
> **No Client Work Without Engagement.**
>
> **No Access Without Authority.**
>
> **No Change Without Record.**
>
> **No Production Change Without Recovery.**
>
> **No Commitment Beyond Authority.**
>
> **No Incident Without Disclosure.**
>
> **No Partner Above Policy.**
>
> **Everything Material Leaves Evidence.**

The economic compact is: **Originate. Serve. Build the Firm.**

The continuity compact is: **Clients belong to the Firm. Partnership authority is earned, not inherited automatically. Founder rights protect the institution, not routine operations.**

When speed and control appear to conflict, the Firm shall use defined emergency authority, preserve a record, disclose consequences, and perform retrospective review. Urgency is not permission to erase accountability.
