# Law-Firm and SRE Model

The law-firm model contributes client acceptance, conflicts discipline, engagement letters, a single accountable Partner, controlled authority, professional judgment, confidentiality, and institutional continuity.

The SRE model contributes least privilege, observable systems, reversible change, incident command, blameless learning, continuity testing, explicit service objectives, and durable operational evidence.

Together they require:

- an accepted Client before work begins;
- an approved Engagement defining scope and responsibility;
- exactly one Responsible Partner for each material Engagement;
- authority grants rather than status-based access;
- recorded, risk-classified changes with recovery plans;
- disclosed incidents with owned communications and corrective action;
- proof that approvals and controls occurred.

Professional judgment may alter the route through an emergency, but it never makes the event invisible. Expedited action must be recorded and reviewed.
