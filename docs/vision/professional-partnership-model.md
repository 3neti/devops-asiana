# Professional Partnership Model

The working legal form is a Philippine General Partnership operated as a professional firm. Its Partnership Agreement will be the constitution; the Managing Partner will conduct ordinary operations within delegated authority; and Founding Partner protections will focus on Reserved Matters.

Ownership, governance rights, professional authority, compensation rights, capital accounts, engagement responsibility, and titles are distinct. No single percentage represents them. An Equity Partner may hold capital without authority to issue a guarantee. A Service Partner may receive substantial delivery credit without constitutional voting rights. A Founding Partner may retain Reserved Matter rights without controlling routine delivery.

The Firm, not an individual Partner, owns client relationships, engagement records, work product subject to contract, operating methods, and Firm assets. Attribution to Originating, Relationship, Responsible, and Engagement Partners is preserved for accountability and compensation.

Partnership authority is personal and earned. Economic value may survive retirement, incapacity, or death, but professional and governance authority does not transfer automatically to an heir.
