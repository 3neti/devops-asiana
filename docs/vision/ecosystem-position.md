# Ecosystem Position

## Roles

**3neti** owns and develops underlying technology and intellectual property.

**ODTI** commercializes, licenses, operates, or represents the platform or scheme ecosystem.

**Institutional customers** own their regulated business, customer relationships, risk acceptance, and normally their infrastructure accounts, domains, DNS, databases, credentials, production data, backups, and direct provider billing.

**DevOps Asiana** provides professional technology operations under a defined Engagement and delegated authority.

These roles must remain contractually, operationally, and evidentially distinct. Related-party relationships among the organizations require disclosure, conflict review, and terms demonstrably appropriate for the Firm and Client.

An Engagement must identify which party owns each asset, who may authorize action, which provider relationships apply, how data may be handled, and which party communicates during incidents. DevOps Asiana's technical ability to act does not establish legal or commercial authority to act.
