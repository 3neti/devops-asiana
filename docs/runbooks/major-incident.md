# Runbook: Major Incident

**Governing policy:** Incident Management; Business Continuity and DR

**Prerequisites:** Major Incident declared

1. Incident Commander establishes objectives, roles, decision log, timeline, and update cadence.
2. Protect people and data, contain spread, and preserve evidence.
3. Technical Lead coordinates diagnosis and recovery; parallel work has named owners.
4. Communication owner issues authorized, factual updates to Clients and internal stakeholders.
5. Responsible Partner evaluates contract, executive, regulatory, insurance, and counsel escalation.
6. Verify recovery, monitor stability, and declare service restoration separately from Incident closure.
7. Schedule a blameless post-incident review and corrective actions.

**Completion:** stable service and stakeholder communication are confirmed; evidence, review, and corrective-action ownership are recorded.
