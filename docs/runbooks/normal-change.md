# Runbook: Normal Change

**Governing policy:** Change Management

**Prerequisites:** approved Change Record with risk, technical review, implementation, recovery, communications, and required Client and Firm approval

1. Conduct a pre-change check of authority, dependencies, monitoring, recovery, and participants.
2. Record start and execute the approved plan, preserving material output.
3. Stop at defined hold points or when assumptions fail.
4. Invoke recovery when trigger conditions occur; do not improvise irreversible scope expansion.
5. Verify technical health and Client-visible outcome separately from deployment.
6. Communicate result and record follow-up actions.

**Completion:** outcome is verified, evidence complete, and required post-implementation review is scheduled or waived by policy-based criteria.
