# Runbook: Standard Change

**Governing policy:** Change Management

**Prerequisites:** active Engagement; approved standard-change definition; eligible target; authorized operator; current recovery method

1. Link the Change Record to the approved standard definition and target.
2. Confirm eligibility, maintenance window, monitoring, backup or recovery readiness, and absence of conflicting activity.
3. Execute the approved steps without material variation.
4. Capture deployment evidence and verify the defined technical and service outcomes.
5. Recover or escalate if any eligibility condition or verification fails.
6. Close with outcome, timestamps, operator, evidence, and deviations.

**Completion:** verification passes and evidence is attached; any deviation is reclassified and reviewed.
