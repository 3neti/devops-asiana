# Runbook: Security Incident

**Governing policy:** Information Security; Incident Management

**Authorized roles:** Incident Commander, Security Lead, Responsible Partner, counsel or privacy roles as designated

1. Declare and restrict the response channel to necessary participants.
2. Preserve logs, system state, identities, and chain-of-custody information before destructive remediation where safe.
3. Contain compromised accounts, hosts, secrets, or data paths using authorized emergency action.
4. Determine affected data, Clients, systems, period, and plausible exposure without overstating certainty.
5. Coordinate legal, contractual, regulatory, insurer, Client, and law-enforcement decisions through competent authority.
6. Eradicate, recover, verify, monitor, and rotate credentials.
7. Preserve the timeline and schedule review and corrective actions.

**Completion:** containment and recovery are verified, notification decisions are recorded, and retained evidence is protected.
