# Runbooks

Runbooks direct time-sensitive technical execution. They remain subordinate to the relevant policy, Engagement, approved Change, and authority grant.

Operators shall record material timestamps, actors, commands or automation references, outputs, decisions, approvals, verification, and deviations. A runbook may accelerate safe action; it may not manufacture missing authority.

The seeded runbooks cover production change, release, incidents, credentials, backups, and restore testing. Client- and platform-specific commands belong in later controlled variants linked to the applicable Engagement.
