# Runbook: Emergency Change

**Governing policy:** Change Management; Incident Management

**Authorized roles:** Incident Commander or emergency Change authority, authorized operator, Responsible Partner

1. Declare the emergency and create or identify the contemporaneous Change Record.
2. State harm being prevented, authority used, scope, risk, and known recovery option.
3. Obtain the fastest required independent approval available; record if emergency authority permits action without it.
4. Execute the minimum change needed to stabilize or contain.
5. Verify, communicate, preserve evidence, and rotate emergency credentials if used.
6. Schedule retrospective review and formalize corrective work as separate records.

**Completion:** the condition is stabilized, stakeholders are informed, evidence is preserved, and retrospective review has an owner and deadline.
