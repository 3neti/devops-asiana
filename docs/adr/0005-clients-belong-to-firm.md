# ADR 0005: Clients Belong to the Firm

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-17

## Context

Personal ownership of Client relationships weakens continuity and encourages fragmented risk and records.

## Decision

The Firm owns Client relationships while preserving Originating, Relationship, Responsible, Engagement, and delivery attribution.

## Consequences

Departure or death does not automatically move a Client. Compensation may recognize contribution, but Client custody, records, and transition remain institutional.
