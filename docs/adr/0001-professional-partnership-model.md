# ADR 0001: Model DevOps Asiana as a Professional Partnership

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-17

## Context

The Firm must accept institutional responsibility for critical operations while preserving personal professional accountability and long-term stewardship.

## Decision

Model DevOps Asiana as a Philippine General Partnership with professional-firm language, a Managing Partner for ordinary operations, Partner classes, and constitutionally bounded Reserved Matters.

## Consequences

Clients and Firm assets are institutional. Admission and authority are earned. General Partnership liability, governance mechanics, and final language require Philippine counsel.
