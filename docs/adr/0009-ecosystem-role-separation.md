# ADR 0009: Keep 3neti, ODTI, Customer, and Firm Roles Separate

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-17

## Context

The ecosystem contains related but legally and operationally distinct owners, commercial actors, customers, and operators.

## Decision

Represent 3neti technology/IP, ODTI platform/commercialization, Customer asset and risk ownership, and DevOps Asiana delegated operations as distinct roles.

## Consequences

Engagements must identify asset ownership and authority. Related-party dealings require conflict review. Technical access cannot blur commercial, regulatory, or legal responsibility.
