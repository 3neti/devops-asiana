# ADR 0012: Separate Roles, Offices, People, and Personal Rights

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-17

## Context

Responsibilities and authority must survive a change in office holder, while some constitutional rights may remain personal to Founding Partner status.

## Decision

People hold Roles and Management Offices; Roles carry responsibilities; delegated authority may attach to an office; and personal constitutional rights attach separately to Partner status.

## Consequences

Managing Partner authority can transfer without rewriting every responsibility. Founding Partner rights are not inferred from management office, and responsibility coverage can be validated independently.
