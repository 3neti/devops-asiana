# ADR 0004: Separate Ownership, Governance, Authority, and Compensation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-17

## Context

A single Partner percentage cannot safely express capital, votes, authority, engagement accountability, title, and compensation.

## Decision

Represent Capital Interest, Governance Rights, Professional Authority, Compensation Rights, Capital Account, role, and Engagement responsibility separately.

## Consequences

Partner records will require explicit related grants and entitlements. Ownership never implies unlimited signing, spending, or production authority.
