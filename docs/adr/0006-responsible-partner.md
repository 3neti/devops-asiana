# ADR 0006: Require Exactly One Responsible Partner

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-17

## Context

Shared delivery can make professional accountability ambiguous.

## Decision

Every material active Engagement must have exactly one current Responsible Partner, with historically preserved handovers.

## Consequences

Work may be delegated widely, but accountability has one answer. Incapacity, departure, or reassignment requires an explicit transition rather than an overwritten field.
