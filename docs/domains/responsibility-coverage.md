# Responsibility Coverage

Responsibility Coverage answers whether each required office, professional responsibility, or authority resolves to qualified human coverage. It is a projection of the Partnership definition and exact policy versions, not a second source of Partner status, office appointments, or delegation.

## Requirement anatomy

Each requirement identifies its category, criticality, governing source, holder source, authority attachment, holder cardinality, qualification, responsibilities, concentration-review treatment, and succession expectation.

Authority attachment is explicit:

- **Office** authority follows a valid appointment to an office such as Managing Partner.
- **Partner status** carries only the personal constitutional rights stated by the governing instrument.
- **Professional role** identifies accountability or capability but does not imply power to bind the Firm.
- **Delegation** requires a distinct authority grant; title, access, ownership, and prior action are not substitutes.
- **None** means that the assignment carries responsibility without separate decision authority.

## Coverage states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Covered | The operative requirement has enough known, qualified holders within its cardinality. |
| Vacant | An operative requirement has fewer qualified holders than required. |
| Pending activation | The requirement is disclosed by a policy version that is not yet Effective. It is not a live vacancy or authority grant. |
| Conflicted | The requirement has invalid sources, unknown or unqualified holders, incompatible attachment, or excess holders. |

Qualification, separation, concentration, and succession remain separate reports. An assignment can exist without being qualified. Current coverage can exist without a distinct alternate. Concentration review is a continuity signal and does not silently revoke valid authority.
