# Partnership Economics Domain

The economics domain distinguishes Capital Interest, Capital Account, Compensation Right, Governance Right, Profit Distribution, Firm Capital, and Engagement contribution attribution.

Client revenue belongs to the Firm. A future contribution statement may derive Contribution Margin from collected revenue and defined direct costs, then attribute Origination, Relationship, Responsible Partner, Delivery, Management, Practice, Mentoring, and Firm-building contributions under an approved policy. Final formulas and percentages remain unresolved.

The Firm Pool supports reserves, people, insurance, tools, facilities, training, and institutional development. Assets acquired by the Firm remain Firm assets and are not partitioned casually by Partners.

Departure events require separate treatment of accrued compensation, capital account, redemption value, continuing economic rights if any, and termination of professional authority. Legal, tax, valuation, and accounting design require counsel and professional advisers.
