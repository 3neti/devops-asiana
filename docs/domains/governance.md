# Governance Domain

Governance records who may decide, what they decided, and how the decision may be executed. Partner class, office, ownership, and delegated authority are separate inputs.

Core concepts are Partner, Partner Class, Governance Right, Reserved Matter, Meeting, Proposal, Conflict Declaration, Vote, Decision, Delegation, Authority Grant, Authority Matrix Entry, and Approval.

The model must support ordinary and Reserved Matters; individual and collective authority; thresholds; time-bound delegation; recusal; emergency decisions; and later ratification where legally available. It must not infer a decision from signatures, payments, deployments, or other execution events.

Every material decision should answer: what was proposed, who was eligible, who was conflicted, what threshold applied, what was decided, when it became effective, who may execute it, and where the evidence resides.
