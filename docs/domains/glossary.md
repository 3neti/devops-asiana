# Domain Glossary

These terms describe the business domain. They do not prescribe Laravel class or table names.

| Term                   | Meaning                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Firm                   | DevOps Asiana as an institution distinct from any individual Partner                              |
| Partnership            | The legal and professional association constituted by the Partnership Agreement                   |
| Partner                | A person formally admitted under the Partnership Agreement                                        |
| Founding Partner       | Original constitutional steward with expressly defined rights                                     |
| Equity Partner         | Partner with capital participation and defined governance rights                                  |
| Service Partner        | Senior Partner participating in service economics without necessarily holding full capital rights |
| Principal              | Senior non-Partner professional able to lead within delegation                                    |
| Associate              | Professional staff member working within supervision and authority                                |
| Managing Partner       | Partner delegated ordinary management of the Firm                                                 |
| Practice               | Professional capability classification, not necessarily a department                              |
| Client                 | Person or organization formally accepted by the Firm                                              |
| Prospective Client     | Candidate not yet accepted as a Client                                                            |
| Engagement             | Approved boundary for material Client work                                                        |
| Responsible Partner    | The one Partner professionally accountable for a material Engagement                              |
| Originating Partner    | Person credited with introducing or securing the relationship or work                             |
| Relationship Partner   | Person stewarding the ongoing Client relationship                                                 |
| Engagement Partner     | Partner leading defined engagement delivery, if distinct                                          |
| Technical Lead         | Person directing technical execution within delegated authority                                   |
| Authority              | Institutional permission to decide, commit, approve, or act                                       |
| Delegation             | Bounded transfer of authority from a competent grantor                                            |
| Reserved Matter        | Constitutional decision requiring special approval                                                |
| Approval               | Affirmative authorization by competent authority                                                  |
| Decision               | Institutional choice, distinct from its execution                                                 |
| Policy                 | Mandatory control requirements deriving authority from the constitution                           |
| Policy Exception       | Temporary approved deviation from a specific requirement                                          |
| Procedure              | Repeatable process implementing policy                                                            |
| Runbook                | Operational instructions for execution or response                                                |
| Control                | Measure designed to prevent, detect, correct, or evidence risk handling                           |
| Evidence               | Durable proof supporting a material fact or control event                                         |
| Audit Trail            | Ordered, attributable history of material state and action                                        |
| Production Environment | Live environment whose behavior or data affects real operations or Clients                        |
| Access Grant           | Recorded authority for a named identity to access a defined resource                              |
| Privileged Access      | Access capable of material security, availability, data, or control impact                        |
| Change                 | Intentional alteration to a managed system or service                                             |
| Emergency Change       | Expedited Change necessary to prevent or contain material harm                                    |
| Incident               | Unplanned degradation, interruption, security concern, or material operational risk               |
| Major Incident         | Incident requiring coordinated command because of impact or complexity                            |
| Security Incident      | Incident involving suspected compromise of confidentiality, integrity, availability, or control   |
| Corrective Action      | Owned work addressing an identified contributing condition or control gap                         |
| Capital Account        | Record of a Partner's capital position, distinct from compensation                                |
| Origination Credit     | Compensation attribution for bringing work to the Firm                                            |
| Delivery Credit        | Compensation attribution for performing or leading Client service                                 |
| Firm Pool              | Contribution retained or allocated for shared institutional purposes                              |
| Partner Compensation   | Economic reward for contribution, distinct from capital                                           |
| Firm Capital           | Assets and retained resources owned by the Firm                                                   |
| Conflict               | Interest or duty that may impair or appear to impair professional judgment                        |
| Related Party          | Person or entity connected in a way requiring disclosure or enhanced review                       |
