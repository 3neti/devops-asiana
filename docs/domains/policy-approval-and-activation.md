# Policy Approval and Activation

A controlled policy document is not operative merely because it exists in the repository. The Firm shall preserve four independent institutional facts:

```text
Effective Firm Decision Record
        │
        ▼
Policy Approval Admission
        │
        ▼
Exact-content Publication
        │
        ▼
Policy Activation and Effective Date
```

## Approval admission

An eligible Decision Record must be institutionally valid, effective, approved, and explicitly reference one exact Policy Version. Its admission record preserves an immutable snapshot of the decision title, context, outcome, decision and effective times, Decision Evidence, and authority basis. The admission identifies one target Policy Version, recorder, recording time, and Evidence.

A Decision candidate does not approve policy by itself. Duplicate admission of a Decision or Policy Version and any contradiction with the source Decision are conflicts.

## Publication

Publication identifies the exact controlled document path and content digest, publisher, publication time, and Evidence. Publication does not supply approval and does not make the policy effective.

## Activation

Activation cites the exact approval admission and publication for the same Policy Version. It records the effective date, activating identity, recording time, and Evidence. The Policy Version's declared effective date must match the activation record.

Approval without publication is not activation. Publication without approval is not activation. Activation cannot be inferred from Git history, document rendering, operational use, or authentication to the Console.

## Foundational bootstrap boundary

Ordinary policy approval consumes authority created by the Partnership constitution and operative governance controls. The first activation of foundational governance and authority policies cannot circularly rely on those same policies to validate their own approval. Formation ratification or another counsel-reviewed constitutional bootstrap mechanism must be modeled explicitly; the compiler shall not invent one.
