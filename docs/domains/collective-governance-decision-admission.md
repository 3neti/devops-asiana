# Collective Governance Decision Admission

An adopted Governance Meeting outcome is not itself a canonical Decision Record. It is an eligible source that may be admitted through an explicit institutional record.

## Control chain

```text
Concluded Governance Meeting
        │
        ▼
Eligible agenda-item candidate
        │
        ▼
Collective Admission Record
        │
        ▼
Canonical Decision Record
```

The admission shall identify one Meeting, one agenda item, and one target Decision Record. It shall preserve an exact snapshot of the title, materiality, participating Partners, weighted vote, Authority Matrix entry, decision time, outcome Evidence, and all source Evidence references. It shall also identify who recorded the admission, when, and under which Evidence Record.

Admission supplies a collective approval basis only. It does not create the target Decision Record, make a proposal complete, activate a decision, authorize execution, or prove verification. The target Decision Record must independently satisfy its governing policies, review, risk, lifecycle, effective-period, and Evidence controls.

Duplicate admission of one source or one target is a conflict. A snapshot that differs from the resolved Meeting source is a contradiction. A Meeting candidate that has not been explicitly admitted remains visible but grants no approval basis.

Single-holder authority remains a separate path. A Managing Partner or other effective holder continues to act only through the exact Authority Matrix entry applicable to that individual or office.
