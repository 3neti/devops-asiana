# Evidence Domain

Evidence links institutional assertions to durable proof. The core concepts are Evidence Record, Source, Actor, Custodian, Subject, Assertion, Approval Reference, Integrity Metadata, Classification, Retention Rule, Legal Hold, Supersession, and Correction.

Evidence may belong to the Client, Firm, or another party even when the Firm maintains its index. Ownership, operational custody, and access are distinct.

High-value queries should traverse domain links rather than rely on free text: the Firm must be able to move from a production action to its Change, Engagement, Responsible Partner, authority, approval, exception, verification, Client communication, and corrective action.

Historical records should be append-only after finalization. Corrections and redactions must be attributable, authorized, and non-destructive to required history.
