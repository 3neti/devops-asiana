# Governance Meetings and Partner Votes

## Purpose

The Governance Meeting Record is the Firm's durable account of collective Partnership deliberation. It answers who received notice, who attended, whether quorum existed, what conflicts were disclosed, who recused, how each eligible Partner voted or abstained, what outcome followed from the applicable constitutional rule, and where the Evidence resides.

Meeting administration does not create governance authority. Governance weight is derived from Partnership Formation. Participation authority is resolved through the Authority Matrix. Quorum, approval thresholds, and deadlock mechanics must come from resolved constitutional truth.

## Institutional chain

```text
Partnership Formation
        ↓
Governing Partners + governance weight
        +
Resolved constitutional quorum / approval / deadlock rules
        +
Effective collective Firm Authority
        ↓
Notice → Attendance → Quorum
        ↓
Proposal → Conflict disclosure → Recusal
        ↓
Vote / opposition / abstention
        ↓
Derived outcome + Evidence
        ↓
Candidate Decision Record
```

Attendance is not consent. Silence is not a vote or abstention. Abstention is an attributable vote state but is never affirmative support. Recusal is agenda-item specific and does not erase the historical attendance record. Execution does not ratify an invalid or undocumented vote.

## Meeting and item records

A Meeting Record preserves lifecycle, schedule, convening and conclusion times, chair, secretary, notice and agenda circulation, attendance for every governing Partner, agenda items, and minutes Evidence.

Each agenda item preserves classification, exact Reserved Matter reference where applicable, proposal, risk, conflict declarations, related-party disclosure, recusals and reasons, attributable votes, recorded outcome, outcome time, authority source, and Evidence. Governance weight is never accepted from the Meeting Record itself.

## Outcome discipline

The compiler derives the outcome from resolved rules and weighted votes, then compares it with the recorded outcome. A contradiction is a conflict. An adopted outcome may emit a candidate Decision Record projection only when the meeting is Concluded, governing policies are Effective, collective authority covers every voter, quorum and approval mechanics are resolved, all required records are complete, and Evidence is present.

The candidate is not a canonical Decision Record and does not modify `resources/institution/decision-records.json`. Collective admission into that ledger requires a subsequent explicit compiler boundary.

## Truthful initial state

The canonical register contains no Meeting Records. Known formation decisions are not reconstructed as minutes. The live definition exposes these unresolved questions:

- ordinary-matter quorum;
- ordinary-matter approval threshold and denominator;
- Reserved Matter quorum;
- Reserved Matter approval threshold and denominator; and
- the 50/50 deadlock mechanism.

The Partnership Governance and Authority & Delegation policies remain Draft. Founding Partner Reserved Matter participation is not yet an effective Authority Matrix entry. No current vote can therefore produce an operative Partnership decision.

## Counsel boundary

Philippine counsel must validate statutory meeting requirements, permissible voting and quorum rules, conflict and recusal effects, Reserved Matter enforceability, deadlock remedies, authority to bind the General Partnership, and the formal legal effect of minutes and written consents. This compiler records institutional intent and consistency; it does not claim legal validity.
