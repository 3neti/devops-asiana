# Institutional Architecture

```text
Partnership Agreement (constitution)
  └─ Policies (mandatory control outcomes)
      └─ Procedures and runbooks (authorized execution)
          └─ Evidence and audit trails (proof)
```

The chain operates in both directions. Authority flows downward; evidence and accountability flow upward. An operational record should identify its Engagement, governing policy version, procedure or runbook, authority, approvals, actors, verification, and evidence.

## Professional accountability

```text
Firm
  └─ Client Acceptance
      └─ Engagement
          ├─ exactly one Responsible Partner
          ├─ delegated operating authority
          ├─ Access / Changes / Incidents / Continuity
          └─ evidence
```

This structure prevents informal relationships or technical capability from becoming authority. Exceptions remain visible branches from a particular requirement, with compensating controls and expiry; they do not rewrite policy.
