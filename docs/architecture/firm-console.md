# Firm Console

The Firm Console is the read-only institutional interface of DevOps Asiana. It exists to inspect responsibility, authority, uncertainty, and evidence—not to display generic KPIs or edit canonical prose.

## Firm Map

The primary control surface projects one resolved institutional model through four views:

- **Partnership:** Partner Status, governance, constitutional position, and Firm continuity;
- **Management:** offices, current holders, office responsibilities, and authority basis;
- **Responsibilities:** assigned and unassigned institutional accountabilities; and
- **Economics:** Engagement Contribution allocations, visibly separate from governance and capital.

Partners are selectable because accountability must resolve to people. The Firm Allocation is visible but never rendered as a Partner.

## Document browser

The sidebar catalogue is generated from the `docs/` hierarchy and each document's first heading. Documents are rendered as sanitized Markdown and remain read-only. Agents modify canonical repository artifacts; people review them through the Console and normal source-control review.

## Control direction

The console should evolve toward answering, with reasons: who is responsible, what authority exists, what Client Mandate applies, what risk is accepted, which approval governs, what occurred, who verified it, and where evidence resides.

It must not become a generic CRM, GRC suite, ticketing tool, rich-text editor, or generalized workflow engine.
