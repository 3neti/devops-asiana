# Risk Transfer and Insurance

The Firm's risk philosophy is:

```text
Prevent → Control → Evidence → Contractually Allocate → Insure Residual Risk
```

Insurance does not eliminate General Partnership liability and must not substitute for competence, authority, access control, recovery, disclosure, or contractual discipline.

The Firm should obtain professional advice on Professional Indemnity / Errors & Omissions, Cyber Liability, General Liability, Property and Equipment, Fidelity or Employee Dishonesty, and other management or specialty coverages. Evaluation should address limits, exclusions, deductibles, retroactive dates, territorial scope, regulated institutions, subcontractors, contractual liability, notification duties, and continuity.

Client contracts should receive controlled review for indemnities, liability caps, warranties, service credits, data obligations, restoration promises, third-party failures, and insurance terms. Coverage and contract positions remain legal and commercial decisions, not assumptions encoded by engineers.
