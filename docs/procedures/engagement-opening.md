# Procedure: Engagement Opening

**Governing policy:** Engagement; Authority and Delegation; Client Acceptance

**Authorized roles:** Responsible Partner, commercial reviewer, delegated Engagement approver

## Prerequisites

The Client has a current acceptance and the proposed service is within any acceptance conditions.

## Procedure

1. Define scope, exclusions, service levels, pricing, duration, transition, responsibilities, and dependencies.
2. Name exactly one Responsible Partner and identify attribution and delivery roles.
3. Inventory covered systems, environments, asset ownership, data classifications, authorized requestors, and escalation contacts.
4. Define access, Change, Incident, communication, provider, subcontractor, insurance, and evidence requirements.
5. Review commercial commitments and exceptions against the Authority Matrix.
6. Obtain Client agreement and internal approval from competent authority.
7. Assign a unique Engagement identifier and communicate the approved operating boundary.

**Evidence:** accepted Client link, executed engagement instrument, internal approval, authority and exception records.

**Escalation:** unresolved ownership, liability, regulatory, staffing, security, or authority issues prevent opening.

**Completion:** the Engagement is approved, effective, staffed, and its operating boundary is available to the Service Team.
