# Procedure: Corrective Action Management

**Governing policy:** Authority and Delegation; the policy governing the source finding

**Authorized roles:** finding owner or reviewer, Responsible Partner where Client work is involved, delegated assigning authority, accountable owner, independent verifier, closure authority

## Prerequisites

- a durable source record or independently evidenced finding;
- an exact governing policy version, requirement, and affected control;
- an identified risk owner and authorized assignment path.

## Procedure

1. Preserve the source finding, actor, time, reason, and evidence without changing the source record's meaning.
2. State the risk, impact, residual risk if delayed, governing requirement, and affected control.
3. Assign exactly one accountable owner under recorded authority and obtain explicit acceptance.
4. Define the remediation outcome, acceptance criteria, steps, dependencies, and initial due date.
5. Record progress and blockers as attributable, append-only updates linked to evidence.
6. Record any due-date change as a new authorized, reasoned, evidenced entry; retain every earlier date.
7. Escalate overdue or materially blocked work without treating escalation as an implicit extension or risk acceptance.
8. Let the accountable owner submit a completion claim against the acceptance criteria with evidence.
9. Have a qualified person other than the owner independently verify the declared outcome and record the observed result.
10. Obtain a separate authorized closure decision after successful verification, or an explicit evidenced cancellation or supersession decision where applicable.

**Required approvals:** assignment, any due-date change, material risk acceptance or Policy Exception, cancellation or supersession, and closure according to the Authority Matrix.

**Evidence:** source finding, assignment acceptance, due-date history, progress, escalation, completion claim, verification, and closure or other disposition.

**Escalation:** overdue work and material blockers go to the Responsible Partner and applicable risk or delegated authority. Client, legal, regulatory, or insurer communication follows the Engagement and governing policy.

**Completion:** the Corrective Action is closed only after independent verification succeeds and competent authority records closure with evidence. Source closure is never a substitute.
