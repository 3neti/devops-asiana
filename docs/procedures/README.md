# Procedures

Procedures implement policy through repeatable administrative and control processes. They do not create authority or waive policy.

Every procedure shall identify governing policy, purpose, prerequisites, authorized roles, steps, approvals, evidence, escalation, and completion. Local Client variations must be linked to the Engagement and may not silently weaken the governing requirement.

Initial procedures are drafts for process design. The seeded set covers Client and Engagement opening, access governance, corrective-action accountability, people offboarding, Client closure, and vendors. Future candidates include new cloud account, new production environment, policy approval, policy exception, partner admission, training records, and financial approvals.
