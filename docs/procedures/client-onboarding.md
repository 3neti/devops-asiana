# Procedure: Client Onboarding

**Governing policy:** Client Acceptance; Information Security; Professional Conduct

**Authorized roles:** acceptance reviewer, Responsible Partner candidate, delegated Client acceptance approver

## Prerequisites

A Prospective Client record, identified beneficial ownership, proposed services, and designated acceptance reviewer exist.

## Procedure

1. Verify identity, ownership, regulatory status, and authorized contacts.
2. Perform and document conflict and related-party checks, including ODTI and 3neti relationships.
3. Assess reputation, technical and security posture, access and data needs, staffing, commercial risk, liability, insurance, and applicable outsourcing requirements.
4. Record risks, conditions, unresolved questions, and proposed acceptance validity.
5. Obtain an explicit decision from delegated authority.
6. Notify relevant personnel of acceptance, conditional acceptance, or rejection without exposing restricted review material.

**Evidence:** Client Acceptance Record, check results, risk assessment, conflict disposition, approval and conditions.

**Escalation:** conflicts, sanctions concerns, uncertain regulatory duty, or unacceptable liability go to the Managing Partner and counsel as applicable.

**Completion:** the decision is recorded; acceptance does not itself authorize Client work.
