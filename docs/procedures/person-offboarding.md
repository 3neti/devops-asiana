# Procedure: Employee or Partner Offboarding

**Governing policy:** Production Access; Information Security; Engagement; Partnership Governance

**Authorized roles:** People owner, Security, Responsible Partners, Managing Partner

## Procedure

1. Record effective time, departure type, continuing duties, and authorized coordinators.
2. Reassign Responsible Partner, Client, Engagement, Change, Incident, and Practice responsibilities before departure where possible.
3. Revoke or transfer named accounts, privileged access, physical access, devices, secrets, tokens, and provider permissions at the risk-appropriate time.
4. Rotate shared or emergency credentials the person could access.
5. Preserve Firm and Client records; recover assets without accessing personal material beyond lawful authority.
6. Notify Clients, providers, banks, or counterparties where authority or contract requires.
7. Record unresolved capital, compensation, confidentiality, conflict, insurance, and legal matters separately.

**Evidence:** approvals, access inventory, revocation logs, asset receipt, responsibility transfers, notifications.

**Completion:** operational authority is removed, continuity owners are confirmed, and remaining legal or economic matters have accountable owners.
