# Procedure: Engagement Closure

**Governing policy:** Engagement; Evidence

**Authorized roles:** Responsible Partner, Engagement Partner, evidence custodian

## Procedure

1. Confirm contractual completion or termination and Client acknowledgement where required.
2. Resolve or transfer open deliverables, Changes, Incidents, risks, corrective actions, and commercial items.
3. Revoke Engagement-specific access and close temporary authority grants.
4. Assemble the Engagement evidence index and apply retention and legal-hold requirements.
5. Record performance, lessons, attribution, and any continuing Client relationship responsibilities.
6. Obtain closure approval from the Responsible Partner and any required Client authority.

**Completion:** obligations are complete or explicitly transferred, authority is reconciled, evidence is indexed, and closure is approved.
