# Procedure: Client Offboarding

**Governing policy:** Engagement; Production Access; Information Security

**Authorized roles:** Responsible Partner, Technical Lead, Security, commercial owner

## Procedure

1. Confirm termination authority, effective date, transition obligations, and communication plan.
2. Inventory open Engagements, systems, data, credentials, Changes, Incidents, invoices, Client property, and evidence.
3. Agree a controlled transition, including knowledge transfer and service-risk ownership.
4. Return or transfer Client-controlled assets and securely dispose of Firm-held copies subject to retention duties.
5. Revoke Firm access and verify Client or successor control.
6. Record unresolved obligations, disputes, security matters, and retention holds.

**Evidence:** termination notice, transition plan, transfer receipts, access revocation, disposal or retention record, final acceptance.

**Completion:** no unexplained access or custody remains and all surviving obligations have owners.
