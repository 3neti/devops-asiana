# Procedure: Vendor Onboarding

**Governing policy:** Financial Authority; Information Security; Professional Conduct

**Authorized roles:** sponsor, Procurement or Finance, Security, delegated approver

## Procedure

1. Identify service, beneficial owner, data and systems touched, cost, term, renewal, and exit dependency.
2. Check conflicts and related parties; disclose ODTI, 3neti, Partner, or personnel interests.
3. Assess security, privacy, continuity, subcontracting, financial, reputation, and concentration risk proportionate to use.
4. Review contract authority, liability, data handling, audit, notification, termination, and return or deletion terms.
5. Obtain financial, security, and business approval under applicable thresholds.
6. Record owner, review date, renewal controls, access grants, and evidence.

**Completion:** the vendor is approved, contracted by competent authority, recorded, and assigned an accountable owner.
