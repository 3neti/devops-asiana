# Procedure: Production Access Review

**Governing policy:** Production Access; Information Security

**Authorized roles:** access reviewer, system owner, Responsible Partner, Security

## Procedure

1. Establish the authoritative population of identities and privileges for the review scope.
2. Reconcile accounts to active people, roles, Engagements, and current grants.
3. Identify excessive, dormant, shared, orphaned, expired, emergency, or unexplained access.
4. Require the system owner and Responsible Partner to affirm, reduce, suspend, or revoke access.
5. Escalate material discrepancies and investigate evidence gaps.
6. Verify remediation and record any approved temporary exception.

**Evidence:** population source, reviewer, decisions, removals, exceptions, completion approval.

**Completion:** every in-scope privilege has a recorded disposition and remediation is verified or tracked as an approved exception.
