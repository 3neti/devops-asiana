## Principle

> **Originate. Serve. Build the Firm.**

Partner compensation shall reward contribution without reducing the Firm to pure “eat what you kill.” Client relationships and revenue belong to the Firm, while individual contribution attribution remains visible and economically meaningful.

## Contribution categories

The future framework may recognize Origination Credit; Relationship Management; Responsible Partner accountability; delivery and Engagement leadership; Practice leadership; mentorship; institutional development; Firm management; capital return; and profit distribution. These are separate dimensions and shall not be collapsed into ownership percentage.

The distributable basis should begin with collected Client revenue less defined direct Engagement costs and other approved adjustments. Illustratively, contribution margin may support origination, delivery or engagement credit, and a Firm Pool. No percentage in an illustration is operative policy.

The policy shall eventually define shared Engagements, split attribution, bad debt, discounts, uncollected invoices, write-offs, rework, management contribution, discretion, capital return, reserves, departing Partners, and correction of errors. It shall prevent double counting and preserve the difference between compensation, profit distribution, and capital-account movements.

The Firm shall retain sufficient contribution to build people, tooling, insurance, reserves, facilities, intellectual property, and long-term capability. Compensation decisions shall be documented, conflict-managed, reviewable, and consistent with the Partnership Agreement and applicable tax and legal advice.
