## Purpose

The Firm shall prepare to continue its own critical responsibilities and support Client continuity according to each Engagement. No generic RTO or RPO is implied for a Client.

## Requirements

Continuity plans shall identify critical services, owners, dependencies, provider and regional concentration, alternate personnel, emergency contacts, communication paths, credential continuity, minimum operating resources, and recovery priorities.

Client-specific recovery time objectives and recovery point objectives must be explicit, approved, feasible, and linked to architecture and testing. Backups shall have known ownership, scope, frequency, retention, encryption, monitoring, and restoration procedures. Backup success is not proof of restorability.

The Firm shall conduct risk-based restore tests and continuity exercises. Exercises shall preserve scenario, participants, assumptions, actions, results, gaps, corrective actions, and evidence. Material dependencies on individuals, ODTI, 3neti, cloud providers, telecommunications, or facilities shall be visible.

Runbooks shall be available during the disruption they address. Emergency authority may accelerate response, but action and communications remain attributable and subject to review.
