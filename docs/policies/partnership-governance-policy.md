## Purpose

The Firm shall make decisions through known authority, manage conflicts, and preserve evidence sufficient to establish what was decided and who was authorized to execute it.

## Requirements

Partner meetings shall have an agenda, notice appropriate to the matter, recorded attendance, conflict declarations, quorum determination, motions or proposals, voting result, effective date, assigned executor, and decision record. Minutes shall distinguish discussion from decision.

Ordinary matters may be decided by the Managing Partner, a committee, or Partners according to the Partnership Agreement and Authority Matrix. Reserved Matters require the constitutional approval specified in the Agreement; silence or participation in execution is not consent.

The Firm may delegate defined decisions to committees or officers. Delegation shall state subject, scope, limit, duration, reporting duty, and whether sub-delegation is allowed. Delegation transfers permission to act, not ultimate institutional accountability.

Conflicted persons shall disclose the conflict before deliberation and recuse where required. Related-party transactions require enhanced disclosure, documented fairness review, and approval by disinterested authority.

Emergency governance may shorten notice or permit interim action only under a defined emergency power. The record shall explain urgency, authority relied upon, decision, execution, affected parties, and required ratification or review.

## Decision anatomy

- **Decision:** the institutional choice.
- **Approval:** affirmative authorization by competent authority.
- **Delegation:** a bounded grant permitting another actor to decide or execute.
- **Execution:** the act implementing an approved decision.
- **Evidence:** durable proof of each distinct event.

Governance evidence shall be access-controlled, retained according to schedule, and linked to resulting policies, contracts, authority grants, or actions.
