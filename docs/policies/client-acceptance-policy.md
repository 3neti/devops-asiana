| Version             | 0.1                                        |

## Principle

> **No Client Without Acceptance.**

The Firm shall not commence material work, request privileged access, or make a commercial commitment for a Prospective Client until competent authority accepts the Client and evidence is retained.

## Assessment

Acceptance shall consider identity and beneficial ownership; regulatory status; reputation; conflicts and related parties; applicable sanctions or legal concerns; ODTI and 3neti relationships; technical environment; scope feasibility; security posture; access and data requirements; qualified personnel; commercial viability and payment risk; potential liability; insurance compatibility; subcontractors; and any bank, EMI, privacy, or outsourcing requirements.

The review shall identify unresolved conditions and specify whether they are prerequisites, accepted risks, or grounds for rejection. An acceptance may be conditional, limited to a proposed Engagement, and subject to expiry or re-review.

Client Acceptance does not approve a specific Engagement or authorize work. Engagement approval remains separate. Material changes in ownership, regulatory status, reputation, scope, risk, or payment behavior shall trigger re-evaluation.

The Client Acceptance Record shall identify reviewers, conflicts disposition, risk classification, decision, conditions, approver and authority, date, validity, and supporting evidence. Rejected prospects and reasons shall be protected and retained appropriately.
