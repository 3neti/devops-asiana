# Policy Framework

The policies in this directory are initial institutional drafts. A Git commit does not approve or make a policy effective.

`resources/institution/policies.json` is the machine-readable policy register. It identifies durable policies and versions, while these Markdown files remain the canonical policy content. Once a version enters review, its registered content digest makes silent amendment a compiler conflict. A substantive change then requires a new version rather than replacement of history.

## Required metadata and lifecycle

Every controlled policy version shall identify its owner, approving authority, version, status, approval and effective dates, review frequency, superseded version, references, and approval evidence. The lifecycle is:

```text
Draft → Under Review → Approved → Effective → Superseded → Retired
```

Approved and Effective are separate states: approval authorizes a version; its effective date determines when it governs. Material content is versioned rather than overwritten. Exceptions are explicit, scoped, approved, temporary, and reviewable.

The Firm Console only projects this institutional truth. It does not grant approval authority to an authenticated application user and does not treat browser access as institutional office or Partner status.

## Interpretation

The Partnership Agreement prevails. Policy prevails over procedures and runbooks. The stricter applicable Client contract or legal requirement also applies unless competent authority determines otherwise. Uncertainty is escalated to the policy owner, Responsible Partner, Managing Partner, or counsel as appropriate.
