## Purpose

Firm funds and assets shall be used only for authorized Firm purposes. Firm assets are not personal Partner assets, and capital interest does not entitle a Partner to withdraw or partition specific property.

## Controls

The Authority Matrix shall govern expenditure, procurement, subscriptions and renewals, reimbursements, capital expenditures, bank accounts, payment initiation, discounts, credits, refunds, write-offs, tax payments, reserves, borrowing, guarantees, and related-party spending. Approved thresholds shall define single and dual approval and matters reserved to Partners.

Payments shall have a documented business purpose, correct payee, budget or authority basis, supporting invoice or equivalent evidence, approval distinct from initiation where required, and proof of payment. Bank instructions, borrowing, guarantees, and material changes to recurring obligations require enhanced verification.

Partners and personnel shall not use Firm funds for personal expenditure, obscure the beneficial recipient, divide transactions to avoid thresholds, approve their own unsupported reimbursement, or make unauthorized commitments expecting later ratification.

Client discounts, free services, service credits, indemnities, unlimited liability, security or regulatory warranties, fixed restoration guarantees, and long-duration commitments are commercial risk decisions subject to delegated authority, not informal relationship concessions.

The Firm shall maintain appropriate reserves and distinguish Partner compensation, profit distribution, capital return, and repayment of valid expenses in its records.
