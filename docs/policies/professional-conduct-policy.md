## Standard

The Firm's people shall act with competence, integrity, confidentiality, candor, sound professional judgment, and respect. A Partner is held to a higher standard, not exempted from policy.

Personnel shall accept only work the Firm can competently perform; disclose uncertainty and mistakes; protect Client and Firm information; identify and manage conflicts; document material judgment; escalate risk; communicate accurately; and distinguish facts, estimates, assumptions, and commitments.

## Professional independence

Platform affiliation does not override professional duty to the Client. Relationships with 3neti, ODTI, Partners, or affiliates shall be transparent and governed as potential related-party interests. If a platform release, commercial request, or ecosystem expectation is operationally unsafe or outside Client Mandate, the Firm shall be able to decline or delay action until appropriate authority and conditions exist.

No person may misuse authority, retaliate for good-faith escalation, conceal an Incident, falsify or destroy evidence, make misleading capability or certification claims, issue an unauthorized guarantee, or direct another person to bypass policy silently.

Gifts, hospitality, outside interests, and related-party relationships shall be disclosed and handled under approved thresholds and conflicts procedures. Commercial pressure or seniority does not excuse an unsafe, unlawful, deceptive, or unauthorized act.

Suspected misconduct shall be reported through an available channel that does not require reporting solely to the implicated person. Investigations shall protect fairness, confidentiality, evidence, and non-retaliation while preserving necessary authority to protect Clients and the Firm.
