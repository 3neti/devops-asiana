## Purpose

The Firm shall protect Client and Firm information in proportion to confidentiality, integrity, availability, legal, contractual, and operational risk. This framework does not claim ISO 27001, SOC 2, BSP, PCI, or other certification or compliance.

## Requirements

Information shall be classified, handled, transmitted, stored, retained, and destroyed according to its sensitivity and ownership. Access shall use named identities, MFA where supported, least privilege, managed secrets, periodic review, and prompt joiner/mover/leaver action.

Sensitive information shall use appropriate encryption in transit and at rest. Secrets shall not be committed to source control, embedded in informal messages, or reused without authorization. Devices handling Client information shall meet defined endpoint, patching, encryption, screen-lock, and malware-protection standards.

Secure development shall include controlled repositories, peer review proportionate to risk, dependency management, separated environments, protected deployment paths, and vulnerability handling. Vendor and subcontractor access requires due diligence, contractual obligations, least privilege, logging, and termination controls.

Security-relevant activity shall be logged and preserved consistent with evidence and privacy requirements. Suspected breach, unauthorized disclosure, evidence tampering, or credential compromise shall be treated as a Security Incident and escalated without concealment.

Applicable standards and regulatory requirements shall later be mapped to named controls, owners, evidence, gaps, and remediation rather than represented through unsupported claims.
