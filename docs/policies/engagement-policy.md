## Principle

> **No Client Work Without Engagement.**

Every material piece of Client work shall be represented by an approved Engagement, and every material Engagement shall have exactly one Responsible Partner.

## Minimum definition

An Engagement shall identify the accepted Client; Originating and Relationship Partners where applicable; Responsible Partner; Engagement Partner if distinct; Technical Lead and Service Team; Practice; scope and exclusions; statement of work; service levels; Client and Firm responsibilities; pricing and billing basis; duration, commencement, termination, and transition; authorized requestors; escalation contacts; covered environments and systems; asset ownership; approved access; data classification; change and incident authority; dependencies; providers and subcontractors; insurance requirements; and evidence obligations.

The Responsible Partner owns professional accountability for appropriateness, relationship, staffing, scope, risk, access approvals, material change approvals, incident communication, escalation, commercial commitments, and closure or transition. Tasks may be delegated; accountability remains singular.

Scope, authority, and commercial terms shall not be inferred from technical access or personal relationships. Material amendments require review and approval under the same principles as opening. Work outside scope shall stop or follow an authorized emergency path and be regularized transparently.

Closure shall revoke unnecessary authority, transfer Client assets and records, resolve open changes and incidents, preserve evidence, confirm outstanding obligations, and record final responsibility.
