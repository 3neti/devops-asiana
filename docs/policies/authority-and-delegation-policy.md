## Principle

> **No Commitment Beyond Authority. Ownership does not imply unlimited operational authority.**

No person may bind the Firm, direct use of Client assets, grant access, or communicate an institutional commitment without actual authority for the specific domain and threshold.

## Authority Matrix

The Firm shall maintain a versioned Authority Matrix covering commercial commitments, contract signature, spending, procurement, hiring and termination, banking instructions, borrowing, guarantees, litigation and settlement, production access and changes, credentials, data export, incident declaration, and public, regulatory, or Client incident communication.

Each entry shall define the decision or action, initiator, reviewer, approver, executor, monetary or risk threshold where applicable, prohibited combinations, escalation path, and evidence. Thresholds may be illustrative during design but are not operative until approved.

Authority grants shall be attributable, scoped, time-bound where appropriate, and revocable. Delegates shall not sub-delegate unless expressly permitted. A job title, Partner class, technical access, past practice, Client relationship, or emergency does not by itself confer authority.

High-risk actions should use independent approval where technically and operationally practical. No one person should be able to initiate, approve, execute, and verify a material commitment without an explicit, risk-accepted exception.

Apparent overreach shall be stopped where safe, escalated immediately, and recorded. Ratification, if legally and institutionally available, is a separate affirmative decision; execution never implies approval.
