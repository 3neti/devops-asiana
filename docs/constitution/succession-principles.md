# Succession Principles

> Design principle for counsel; no redemption or inheritance mechanism is settled here.

**Partnership authority is personal. Economic value may survive the Partner.**

On retirement, incapacity, death, expulsion, or other departure, the future Agreement should distinguish accrued compensation, capital account, redemption value, and any time-limited economic participation from voting, Reserved Matter, management, Responsible Partner, and production authority.

An estate or heir should not become a Partner automatically. Founder constitutional rights should normally end or operate under defined personal succession rules rather than become inheritable vetoes.

Every departure must also address Client continuity, reassignment of Responsible Partner duties, credential revocation, records custody, confidentiality, conflicts, pending matters, valuation, payment timing, insurance, and authority notifications. The Firm's Client relationships and assets remain with the Firm subject to final legal instruments.
