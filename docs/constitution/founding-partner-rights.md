# Founding Partner Rights

> Concept for counsel validation.

Founding Partners are original constitutional stewards. Any special rights should protect institutional continuity through Reserved Matters rather than create a parallel approval path for ordinary operations.

Founder protections may address amendment of the Partnership Agreement or founder protections, admission or creation of Partner classes, fundamental ownership changes, merger, sale, dissolution, strategic assets, and succession architecture. The final Agreement must define voting mechanics, duration, waiver, incapacity, retirement, death, conflicts, and whether rights are individual or collective.

Founder rights should generally be personal and non-transferable. Heirs should not inherit vetoes, management office, Responsible Partner authority, or production authority merely through succession. A Founding Partner remains subject to policy, conflicts rules, and delegated-authority limits.
