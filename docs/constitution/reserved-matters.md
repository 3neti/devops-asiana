# Reserved Matters

> Conceptual catalogue; thresholds and enforceability require Philippine counsel.

Reserved Matters are decisions that alter the Firm's constitutional, ownership, risk, or continuity profile. They should include, as appropriate:

- admission of Equity Partners or creation of Partner classes;
- material changes to capital or ownership interests;
- amendment of the Partnership Agreement or founder protections;
- merger, dissolution, sale of substantially all assets, or fundamental change of business;
- major borrowing, guarantees, or security over material Firm assets;
- material related-party transactions;
- disposition or encumbrance of strategic intellectual property;
- change of Firm name;
- material change to Partner compensation architecture;
- appointment or removal of the Managing Partner, if constitutionally reserved;
- admission of a controlling entity; and
- material changes to succession, redemption, or dissolution rights.

The Authority Matrix must distinguish Reserved Matters from ordinary Partner decisions and Managing Partner authority. Each decision requires a proposal, conflict declarations, the required quorum and vote, a decision record, execution authority, and retained evidence.

The Governance Meeting Compiler now enforces exact Reserved Matter classification against this constitutional catalogue and derives vote weight from Partnership Formation. It intentionally cannot produce an outcome until quorum and approval mechanics are resolved. The 50/50 deadlock mechanism remains an explicit unresolved decision subject to counsel review.
