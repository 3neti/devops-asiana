# Partnership Agreement Concept

> Institutional specification for Philippine counsel; not final legal language or legal advice.

The Partnership Agreement should constitute DevOps Asiana as a professional Firm and establish the authority from which policies derive. It should address purpose, term, capital, property, Partner classes, admission, governance, management, duties, conflicts, compensation, distributions, records, authority to bind the Firm, Reserved Matters, transfer restrictions, retirement, incapacity, death, expulsion, valuation, redemption, dissolution, dispute resolution, and amendment.

The instrument should preserve these separations:

- capital interest is not unlimited authority;
- governance rights are not delivery compensation;
- professional title is not ownership;
- technical capability is not delegated authority;
- economic succession is not automatic admission to partnership.

Policies may govern ordinary institutional operations only within the authority delegated by the Agreement. Procedures cannot change policy. Execution cannot cure missing approval. Amendments and waivers must be identifiable and historically preserved.

The Agreement should make Firm property and Client relationships institutional rather than divisible personal property, subject to applicable law and counsel's drafting.
