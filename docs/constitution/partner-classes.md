# Partner Classes and Professional Roles

The Firm may eventually recognize these classes without requiring all of them on day one.

| Classification                     | Institutional meaning                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Founding Partner                   | Original constitutional steward with any expressly defined Reserved Matter rights                               |
| Equity Partner                     | Partner with capital participation and defined governance rights                                                |
| Service Partner                    | Senior professional with participation in engagement or Firm economics, potentially without full capital rights |
| Principal                          | Senior non-Partner able to lead engagements or practices within delegation                                      |
| Associate                          | Professional staff member working under defined supervision                                                     |
| Specialist / Engineer / Consultant | Delivery designation based on professional function                                                             |

A class or title does not itself specify capital, vote, signing authority, system access, compensation, or engagement responsibility. Those arise from separate records and governing instruments.

Potential practice classifications include Cloud & Infrastructure, Platform Operations, Reliability / SRE, Security & Compliance, Banking Integration, and Client Services. They are professional groupings, not necessarily departments.
