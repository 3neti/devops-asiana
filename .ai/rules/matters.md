# Matters

## Matter accountability remains bounded by Engagement
A Matter is a bounded piece of professional work inside an Engagement, not a Client or generic ticket. ResolveMatters requires an open Engagement, exactly one Responsible Partner reconciled with the Engagement, explicit scope, risk ownership and acceptance, escalation, and Evidence before a Matter may be projected as accountable.
