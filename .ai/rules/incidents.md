# Incidents

## Preserve incident accountability boundaries
Incident observations require an explicit declaration; command roles remain distinct from the Engagement Responsible Partner. Client-impacting incidents cannot close without evidenced Client disclosure. Service restoration is a verified operational fact and never implies closure; closure requires final notification decisions, applicable blameless review, owned corrective actions, authority, and evidence.
