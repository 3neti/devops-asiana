# Identity And Roles

## Keep identity roles and authority separate
Institutional Identity is separate from Partner status, employment/service classification, Laravel User authentication, and system accounts. Role Assignments preserve lifecycle, basis, time, and evidence; professional responsibility never grants Firm Authority by itself. Formation-derived assignments remain Approved and non-operative until the Firm effective date is resolved; never invent the date or silently activate them.
