# Institution

## Preserve explicit partnership uncertainty

The repository-backed Partnership Definition is canonical. Keep Partner Status, Governance Weight, Management Office, Operational Authority, Economic Allocation, and Capital Interest separate; never add partner_percentage or infer capital. Unresolved and counsel-review states must remain explicit.

## Institutional purpose is bounded AI-amplified accountability
The canonical purpose states that DevOps Asiana provides professional accountability for AI-amplified technology operations within defined Client Engagements and delegated authority. Preserve the distinction between technical capability, Client Mandate, Firm Authority, Specific Approval, and professional responsibility. This is institutional intent, not legal validity or unlimited liability.
