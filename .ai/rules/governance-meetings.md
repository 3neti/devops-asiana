# Governance Meetings

## Meeting records derive governance; they do not amend it
Derive governing Partners, governance weights, and Reserved Matters from ResolvedPartnership. Quorum, approval thresholds, and deadlock mechanics must remain explicit resolved rules; never infer consent from attendance, silence, abstention, or execution. Recusals are agenda-item specific, and an adopted outcome emits only a non-canonical Decision Record candidate.
