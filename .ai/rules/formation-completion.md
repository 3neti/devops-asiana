# Formation Completion

## Require evidenced Firm commencement
Firm Commencement must reconcile exact Partnership facts with a counsel-confirmed case-specific legal requirement set, executed instrument, separate founder capital references, chronology, and Evidence. Do not encode assumed Philippine registration steps or infer commencement from a date, login, policy state, economics, or observed operations.
