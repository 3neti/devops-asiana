# Institutional Controls

## Control review summarizes source compilers
ResolveInstitutionalControlReview is read-only and cross-domain. It summarizes gaps from Evidence Custody, Retention Reviews, Retention Findings, and Corrective Actions, but never grants authority, accepts risk, creates exceptions, or closes remediation. Source compilers remain canonical.
