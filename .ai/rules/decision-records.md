# Decision Records

## Decisions consume authority; they do not create it
Decision Records are Firm governance/management records only. An operative record must cite one exact effective Authority Matrix entry and effective holder. Keep proposal, review, risk acceptance, decision, execution, verification, and Evidence separate; execution never backfills approval, and Matrix entries requiring Client Mandate are outside this compiler.
