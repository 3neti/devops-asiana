# Continuity Exercises

## Prove continuity through measured recovery
Never invent or infer Client RTO/RPO; each objective must be explicit, sourced, approved, and evidenced. Backup success does not prove restorability. Preserve approved objectives separately from observed recovery time and recovery-point age, prohibit coordinator self-verification, require missed objectives and material gaps to link to canonical Corrective Actions, and keep verification separate from closure.
