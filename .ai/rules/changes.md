# Changes

## Keep Change Decisions and Actions Separate
An Active Access Grant does not authorize a specific Change. Approval does not imply scheduling, scheduling does not prove execution, deployment does not imply approval, and execution does not imply verification or closure. Emergency procedure expedites authority but never waives recovery, disclosure, retrospective review, or evidence.
