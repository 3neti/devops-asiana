# App

## Model institutional rights separately

Future application work must keep capital interest, governance rights, professional authority, compensation rights, role/title, and engagement responsibility separate. Do not introduce a single partner percentage or infer authority from ownership, title, technical access, or execution.

## Build narrow institutional vertical slices

This repository is documentation-first. Implement a narrow domain slice with explicit lifecycle, authority, evidence, and tested invariants; do not introduce generic CRUD, workflow engines, microservices, or speculative integrations before the institutional model requires them.
