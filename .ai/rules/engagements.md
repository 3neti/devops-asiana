# Engagements

## Open Engagements only through the complete gate
Engagements are repository-canonical and cross-resolved against Resolved Client Acceptance, Resolved Partnership, Resolved Policy Registry, and complete Evidence Records. Client work is permitted only for a current Open Engagement with an accepted Client, exactly one evidenced Responsible Partner, bounded Client Mandate, complete terms, explicit Firm approval, and a later Opening Record. Never infer one gate from another or from performed work.

## Keep Engagement risk acceptance explicit
Opening requires an explicit Engagement risk classification, risk owner, accepting actor, authority basis, decision time, and Evidence Record. Do not infer risk acceptance from Engagement approval, Responsible Partner assignment, or operational execution.
