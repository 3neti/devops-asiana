# Authority Matrix

## Resolve only bounded Firm Authority
The Authority Matrix resolves Firm Authority only; it never supplies Client Mandate or Specific Approval. An entry is effective only with an exact operative source, an eligible constitutional status or operative authority-bearing Role Assignment, Active lifecycle, current time boundary, complete scope and thresholds, governing policy, and Evidence. Professional responsibility, login, access, title, ownership, or execution never creates authority; undecided powers and thresholds remain explicit deferred decisions.
