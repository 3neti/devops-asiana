# Corrective Actions

## Keep Corrective Actions independently accountable
A Corrective Action is institutional remediation, not a generic task. Source closure never closes or erases it; require one accountable owner, explicit assignment, append-only due-date history, evidenced overdue escalation, and a governing requirement. The owner may claim completion but may not self-verify; successful independent verification only makes a separate authorized closure decision eligible.
