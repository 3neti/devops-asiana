# Role Transitions Institution Identity And Roles

## Transitions preserve history and expose vacancies
Role transitions are separate admissions over one exact assignment snapshot. Require a competent decision, effective chronology, independent verification, and distinct Evidence. Suspension disables operation; terminal transitions expose a vacancy. A successor declaration never transfers an Office or authority and remains pending separate appointment/admission.
