# Institution Identity And Roles

## Admit each founding role assumption separately
Verified Firm Commencement makes formation-derived assignments eligible but never activates them. Activate only one exact approved assignment through explicit holder acceptance, independent verification by another recognized person, valid chronology, and distinct Evidence for acceptance, verification, and activation. The admission itself grants no Firm Authority; preserve the canonical assignment as Approved and project its effective lifecycle separately.
