# Retention Findings

## Retention findings link to remediation without closing it
ResolveRetentionFindingLinks requires a resolved remediation-bearing Retention Review, an existing Corrective Action, explicit linkage attribution/time/reason, and review-attached Evidence. It never creates, assigns, verifies, or closes Corrective Actions.
